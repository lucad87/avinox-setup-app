🎛️ Mastering the Advanced W/kg Sliders

How to use the manual sliders to mathematically manipulate motor output limits

By expanding the Sliders Avanzati drawer on the tuner screen, you gain granular control over the electrical curves. Each slider represents the desired power target in Watts per Kilogram ($W/kg$) of total system weight. The application processes these thresholds to define specific app limits:

ECO Slider (Range: 0.75 – 2.00 W/kg)

Sets the baseline background support. Dropping it below $1.00\text{ W/kg}$ creates a highly natural "acoustic" feel that demands maximum muscle force but preserves tremendous battery life on flat transit paths.

AUTO Slider (Range: 1.50 – 4.50 W/kg)

Adjusts the top ceiling for the system's dynamic mode. A higher value causes the Avinox smart algorithm to scale up power output much faster on changing slopes, compensating for sudden steep sections.

TRAIL Slider (Range: 2.50 – 7.50 W/kg)

Controls sustained technical singletrack delivery. Keeping this slider bounded between $5.00$ and $5.50\text{ W/kg}$ delivers exceptional technical climbing punch without spinning out or losing tire traction on loose gravel tracks.

TURBO Slider (Range: 3.50 – 10.00 W/kg)

Manages the emergency acceleration ceiling. Critical Guardrail: Sliding this setting (or any other mode) to ≥ 6.00 W/kg activates the High Energy Drain Zone, triggering visual warnings because power draws scale exponentially.

🛠️ Parameters Dictionary & Logic Boundaries

Understanding the core mechanics behind the DJI Avinox control center

Multiplier (W/kg) Primary Input Variable

Determines the core support scaling relative to system weight. Shifting this factor automatically updates the math boundaries across the engine profile limits.

Power Limit (Watts) Battery Protection

The physical limit of ongoing electrical drain. Restricting this limit (e.g., 150W for ECO or 300W for AUTO) is the only definitive way to extend overall structural range.

Max Torque (Nm) Response Character

Specifies raw rotational force. Lower thresholds (20-40 Nm) deliver smooth, authentic pedal matching. Higher bounds (80-120 Nm) introduce aggressive, motorcycle-like engagement.

Assist Level (Effort Multiplier) Bio-Mechanical Match

Dictates how many times the system multiplies your manual input force. Stronger riders require lower configurations, while high-fatigue terrain relies on higher engagement levels.

🚴 Empirical Field Scenarios

Real-world configurations tested on complex vertical elevations

Pure Leg Marathon ECO

Set ECO to 0.85 W/kg with torque clamped at 18 Nm. Forces active human engagement on flat segments, letting the system cross over 160km of range limits safely.

Wet Technical Grip TRAIL

Map TRAIL to 5.00 W/kg but cap torque output at 55 Nm. Prevents rear wheel spinouts on wet root structures or loose rocks while ensuring traction progression.

Vertical Alpine Wall TURBO

Map TURBO to 8.50 W/kg with torque unlocked at 120 Nm. Designed exclusively to defeat brief, nearly impossible vertical pitches where immediate extreme support is mandatory.

The Fundamental Formula of Avinox Systems

The engineering mechanics balancing power delivery

Motor Power (Watts) = (Max Torque [Nm] × Cadence [RPM]) / 9.55

The DJI engine output relies structurally on your pedaling frequency:

⚠️ Lazy Cadence (52 RPM) If you grind a tall gear, the physical output ceiling is electronically clamped at roughly 572 Watts, wasting battery through thermal loss.
✅ Sport Cadence (90 RPM) Spinning an agile gear fully unlocks the architecture's potential, unleashing up to 990 Watts of fluid, uninterrupted electrical support.
