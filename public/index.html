1. The Fundamental Formula of Avinox Systems

Motor Power (Watts) = (Max Torque [Nm] × Cadence [RPM]) / 9.55

The DJI engine does not deliver a static 1000 W output. Power output relies structurally on your pedaling frequency:

  • Lazy Pedaling (52 RPM): The engine physical ceiling is capped at 572 Watts.
  • Radsportler Cadence (90 RPM): Unlocks the system potential up to 990 Watts.
