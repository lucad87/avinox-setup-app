What it does

Two tabs and a drop zone. That's the whole app.

Tuner

Three numbers per mode

Assist level, Max Power and Max Torque for ECO, AUTO, TRAIL and TURBO — sized on your weight, cadence and power. Type them into the DJI app. Done.

Route

Will the battery make it?

Drop a .gpx or .kml: energy verdict with a reserve, grades, climbs, a map coloured by slope and custom modes for that route.

Rides

What you really used

Drop the bike's .proto recordings: real Wh/km, drive efficiency from the battery drop, map and sensor graphs. From then on every estimate is yours.

How to use it

Three steps. The third one is optional, but it's where it gets good.

  1. 1

    Tell it about you

    Motor, battery, your weight and the bike's, cadence and the power you can hold. No idea? Pick a riding style.

  2. 2

    Copy three numbers per mode

    Open the DJI Avinox app, edit each mode, type level, power and torque. The values already sit on the app's 5 Nm / 50 W steps.

  3. 3

    Feed it rides and routes

    Recordings calibrate it on your real consumption. Routes get a verdict before you leave, not halfway up the climb.

Use case 2 · Route

Sunday: 60 km and 1,800 m. Will it make it?

“My friend says it's fine. My friend also said the last one was ‘mostly flat’.”

  1. Export the route from your planner — or from the Avinox app — as .gpx.
  2. Drop it on the Route tab and pick the surface.
  3. Keep a reserve: 15% by default, because walking a 25 kg bike uphill builds character nobody asked for.

Result: an honest verdict with a range, not a single hopeful number — and, when it's tight, a ROUTE RESERVE mode to stretch the last part.

Use case 3 · Rides

Use case 4 · Tuner + Rides

“I pedal like a gentleman”

How much to trust it

We'd rather tell you than have the battery tell you.

Measured

Your rides' consumption, the drive efficiency from the battery drop, distance from the bike's odometer.

Estimated

Ranges, runtimes and route energy — always with a margin. Uncalibrated, expect ±15–35%. A mode you never recorded is a projection.

Not its job

It doesn't talk to the bike: you type the numbers in the DJI app. M1 is not supported. And it can't do the pedalling. Yet.

FAQ

Where do I find the .proto recordings?

On Android, in Android/data/com.avinox.ride/files/ebike/data/ — a folder file managers hide. Copy it over USB, or with the adb pull command the app gives you. The .gpx export of the Avinox app works too, as a route.

Do my files get uploaded?

No. Routes and recordings are read and kept on your device; only the numbers the maths needs — weights, cadence, distance, climbing, grade shares — go to the calculator, never a file or a GPS track. Export saves everything to one file, Import brings it to another browser, Clear all data forgets it all.

Does it work on my phone? Offline?

On the phone, yes: open it in the browser and press Install in the header (on iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen) and it opens full screen, like an app. Offline, no: the calculations and the maps need a connection. Plan at home, ride in the woods.

Why is TURBO so close to TRAIL in kilometres?

The ground decides how much energy a kilometre takes; the mode decides who supplies it. Once the motor does most of the work, more assist costs little per km — you just push less. The difference shows per hour: TURBO climbs faster and empties the battery sooner.

Which motors and batteries?

Avinox M2 and M2S, with the FS600, FP700, FS800, RS600 and RS800 packs. The full 1500 W Boost of the M2S needs the FP700 or the RS800 — the app knows.
